## Decided

- The party is driven in the game in a second tab, not from the 2D layout: the game's Move is `useSessionWalk`, which needs the atlas path index, the turn clock's `declarationId`, `GetWhere` reconciliation and the walk animation's completion, so reusing it from the studio is not a small reuse (the slice's timebox) — [rpg-project#1004](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1004).
- "Open the game for this run" opens the app root in a new tab and keeps only `?playerId`, because resume-after-refresh (#444) seats the player in their running encounter and a `?concept` deep link would hold the view — [rpg-project#1004](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1004).
- One step is one party verb plus everything the world recorded after it, until the next party verb. Nothing moves unless the party acts, so a verb is the natural frame — [rpg-project#1003](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1003).
- A creature's label is the last `state` beat's `to`. Until a `state` beat arrives, it is the role states the last `behavior_decision` left. A later decision never overwrites a state beat — [rpg-project#1004](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1004).
- The live mark is the first box of each machine the creature `runs` whose state id is the label's state or one of its role states. The studio infers nothing else — [rpg-project#1004](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1004).
- Dev gate is `import.meta.env.MODE === 'development'` (the record is served only in development), not `isDevLike`, whose law limits it to named bench gates — [rpg-project#1004](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1004).

## Found

- The record's `moved.position` is the dungeon-absolute AXIAL cell (`x` = q, `y` = r), not an offset pair. It is `HexCellAt`'s output, and the single-room dialect's `{ q, r }` is that cell (`single_room_site.go` `hexCell`). So the studio's draft cell and the record's cell are the same frame, read through the game's `positionToCube` — [rpg-project#1004](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1004).
- `joined` carries `{member, kind}` with no cell, so starting cells come from the draft — [rpg-project#1003](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1003).
- The record feed keeps the newest 500 beats (`RECORD_MAX_ROWS`). In a long run, a creature whose last move aged out is drawn at its draft start until it moves again — [rpg-project#1004](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1004).
