## Shipped

- **A timeline strip under the map.** It spans the map's full width while watching. Each creature has a row and each step a 72 px cell, oldest on the left. The name column stays put when you scroll sideways. At Live the strip scrolls to show the newest step.
- **Two things per step:**
  - The **state band** is coloured by the machine state in force when the step ends. It is read with `foldRun` at the step's last row, the same reading the map gives that step.
  - The **mark** says what the creature did, in the record's own words (`runTimeline.ts`).
- **Edges.** A solid edge marks a state change. A dashed edge marks a change in the kind of thing it did.
- **Click a cell** and the watch shows that step (the same act-id step Back and Forward use) with that creature selected. A vertical line marks the current step across all rows. The arrow keys move along a row.
- **"Only creatures that changed"** is on by default once a run has more than five creatures. The party's rows always show and come first.

## What the record carries for "what it did"

- **`answered`** (`answer.go` `answeredBeatBody`; `clocks.go` `appendPickBeat`) carries `creature`, `word`, `selector`, `key`, `target` and `entry`/`path`:
  - `word` is toward / hold / attack / dash / away / search / flee / fact, or empty for a row that only speaks.
  - `selector` is path / at / the selector word.
  - A machine-picked `key` is qualified as `behavior/<len>:<instance>/<key>`.
  - `say` is blanked here. The words travel only in `speech`.
- **The same body inline** sits on `behavior_decision.selection` when no `answered` beat was written, which happens for a suppressed repeat empty hold (`behavior_trace.go` `appendBehaviorDecision`).
- **`behavior_work`** carries `status` (selected / partial / paused / completed / abandoned), `reason`, and `work{instance, key, kind: arrival|delivered, goal}` (`behavior_work.go` `BehaviorWorkData`).
- **`speech`** carries the `speaker` and `words`.

## Decided

- **The mark is the pick's `word selector`, and the kind it compares also includes the role.** In the castle, a guard's `time` table uses the `guard-base` bundles. Walking the route reads `toward path`, going to a clang reads `toward sound`, and chasing reads `toward enemy`, `attack enemy` or `dash enemy`. In the older capture, a patrol leg (`behavior/4:duty/…`) and an investigation's look (`behavior/11:investigate/look`) are both `toward at`. Telling them apart needs the role that the qualified key names. The `kind` adds it as `(duty)` and the mark leaves it out.
- **The comparison uses the role only when both steps carry one** (review on [#1456](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-dnd5e-web/pull/1456#pullrequestreview-5969057998)). The kind compares `word selector`. The root cause is in the engine: the toolkit writes the inline `behavior_decision.selection` from the pick before it is qualified (`behavior_trace.go:112`), and `appendPickBeat` qualifies only its own copy (`clocks.go:817-819`). So a suppressed repeat hold reads `time`, beside the `answered` hold `behavior/4:duty/time` one step earlier. That is an engine item.
- **Two roles of one creature share one row.** The role only tells changes in what it did apart; the row does not split per role. The band is the body's state (`state`), or the decision's roles label when there is no `state` beat.
- **The selected creature's row always shows**, even under "Only creatures that changed".
- **A recorded `state` beat counts as a change** even when no earlier state was known, because `from` is on the beat.
- **A strip, not a panel section.** The panel is already cramped, and a step axis needs width.

## Found

- **The 500-row record ring covers only a few steps of a castle run.** The capture writes about 12 rows per machine creature per round. With about 10 guards, the ring holds only the last 4 or so steps, so the timeline cannot yet show "patrol → investigate → patrol" over a long run. The fix is web-only: keep a per-step summary that outlives the ring, or give the watch a larger ring.
- **No bundle name is on the record.** An `answered` pick through a `use:` group carries only `entry` and `path` (`[3, 1]`), not the name of the bundle (`patrol`, `alarm-reactor`). The timeline shows the path in its full text. Naming the bundle would be engine work (toolkit `answeredBeatBody`).

## The operator's look: "who held me and why"

He said: "i am trying to see who held me and why when i tried walking away. the capture window deserves enough room."

- **Why the step stopped now stays in view.** The panel's header, step controls and this step's stop are fixed above the scrolling sections, and the same banner sits in the timeline header. What stops a step, per rpg-toolkit:
  - A body, or a crossing a post holds (`step.go`, `barred.go`). The step answers `Stopped{Barrier, Cell, Reason}`, and the api's Move `stopReason` is that sentence. The record writes `barred {mover, barrier, cell, door?, verb?}`, so who held the way is on the record. For a held crossing it is the nearest holder the mover sees.
  - An address at the end of a step (`address.go`). The record writes `addressed {interaction, member, guard}`, so who addressed whom is on the record. This does not refuse a one-hex studio step.
  - A wall, a shut door or a fight. Only the refusal sentence comes back; `barred.go` writes no beat for these by design.
- **The trace pops out.** Expand opens it at 85vw × 85vh, with the step controls at its top. The pop-up is a reusable `StudioDialog`, taken from the Behaviours list pop-up's `.es-dialog` pattern.
- **The inspector is closed while watching**, with its own open state, so N still opens it. The author's own state returns when the watch ends. Closing it the plain way failed: the studio's many `setSidebarOpen(true)` paths reopened it as the watch started.
- **Selecting a creature scrolls its section into view.**
