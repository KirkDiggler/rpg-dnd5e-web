## Decided

- The linked reader validates transport shape, not rule meanings. It does not roll, derive a countdown, add dice, parse opaque effect IDs or reinterpret linked data through cached class-field readers — [design](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/pull/2323).
- Keep independent current Raging and all unmigrated payload readers; no historical linked reader or automatic reset is introduced — [R8](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/pull/2323#issuecomment-6726787390).
