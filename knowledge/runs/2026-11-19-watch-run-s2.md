## Decided

- **All six neighbours are offered, walls and occupied cells included.** The server decides every step and the studio never pre-judges the map. A refused step is the engine's sentence, which is what the operator is there to see — [rpg-project#1006](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1006).
- **`stopReason` and `formed` are handled as answers, not errors.** `MoveResponse` says a short walk is an answer: a stopped step comes back with `stop_reason` and no error, and a fight that started comes back as `formed`. Both are shown; reading only thrown refusals would have shown nothing for them — [rpg-api-protos service.proto MoveResponse](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-api-protos/blob/main/dnd5e/api/session/v1alpha1/service.proto).
- **A `barred` beat's `mover` counts as a party act** (`memberOfRow`). A refused step writes no `moved`, so without this its bumps and the guard's answer fell into the previous step, and Back/Forward could not show the refused attempt — [rpg-project#1006](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1006).
- **The fullest version of a line is used.** The toolkit writes one `speech` beat per group of observers who perceived the same parts, all with one `utterance` id. The dev record holds them all, so the version with the words and the speaker stands for the line. A part no version carries is never invented — [rpg-toolkit utterance.go](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/main/rulebooks/dnd5e/encounter/utterance.go).
- **The tried hex is marked for 3 s** (`TRIED_MARK_MS`): long enough to see where the step went, short enough not to be mistaken for state the record holds. The sentence stays in the panel until the next step — [rpg-project#1006](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1006).
- **No arrow keys.** An axial hex has six neighbours and four arrows, so arrow stepping is not trivial. The six steps are Tab-focusable buttons instead — [rpg-project#1006](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1006).
- **The latch holds until the record moves.** A step that moved holds the busy latch until the record's party steps change, so the next six hexes are drawn around the cell the record now gives. A cap (`RECORD_WAIT_MS`, 1.5 s) means a lost beat never locks stepping. A refused step, which did not move, frees at once — [PR review](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-dnd5e-web/pull/1454#pullrequestreview-5966625349).
- **The panel takes the canvas's right edge, and the map the rest.** The canvas pads its right side by the panel's width, so the map is never drawn under the panel. A machine drawing scaled below 70% stops being readable, so below that it keeps its size and scrolls. Label collisions between nearby creatures are not solved in this slice — [rpg-project#1006](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1006).
- **A refusal belongs to Live.** Stepped back in history, the panel and map show that step's record only — [PR review](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-dnd5e-web/pull/1454#pullrequestreview-5966625349).

## Found

- **The sizing missed answered refusals.** It said refusals would be formatted by `formatMoveError`, but `Move` also answers a stopped step with `stopReason`, or a fight with `formed`, without throwing. Both are now handled — [commit 207499d3](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-dnd5e-web/commit/207499d323a11274badb3d1d5554a90d74a917bc).
- **The empty selector on the turn clock** is refused as `empty declaration id` (ErrNoDeclarationID); a non-active member gets `not your turn` first. Both read as "a fight is on" (`isTurnClockMoveRefusal`, beside `isNotYourTurnError`) — [rpg-toolkit session/move.go](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-toolkit/blob/main/rulebooks/dnd5e/session/move.go).
- **RecordPanel has no speech renderer**: it shows beats as JSON. The game's one speech formatter is `speechWords`, over the act stream's `Speech` message, so the studio builds a `Speech` from the record beat and calls it — [commit e86401b1](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-dnd5e-web/commit/e86401b1b19a5eeaa9e3191c1fdb8ca3bff93496).
- **A `speech` beat names no listener** and the record row carries no audience, so "who heard" cannot be shown from the record — [rpg-project#1006](https://github.com/KirkDiggler/rpg-project/issues/1006).
